A plan keeps its own recipe selection, graph layout and machine grouping only once you change them in its Settings panel. Until then it follows the values below, so changing one here moves every plan that never touched it.
Recipes
Alternate recipes are the ones you unlock from hard drives. With this off, a plan starts with the standard recipes only and you pick the alternates you have in its Recipes tab.
The Converter recipes that turn one raw resource plus Reanimated SAM into another one. With this on, a plan may make scarce ores out of common ones.
Graph layout
Which way the graph flows when it is arranged.
Connections keep the corners they are routed around nodes with, or go straight.
The gap between nodes standing side by side.
The gap between one step of the production chain and the next.
Machine groups
How the machines of a new node are split into groups and clocked. You can change this for each node in the Inspector.
